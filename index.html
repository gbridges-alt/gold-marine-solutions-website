What We Do

Marine Mechanical & Electrical Services

Full-service mobile marine mechanic covering everything from routine outboard services to complex diesel rebuilds.

Outboard Engine Servicing

Annual services, impeller replacements, fuel system repairs, diagnostics. Yamaha, Mercury, Suzuki, Evinrude, Honda.

Inboard Diesel Service

Caterpillar, Cummins, Volvo Penta, Yanmar, Perkins. Full service, diagnostics, cooling system overhauls, rebuilds.

Marine Electrical

Battery systems, rewiring, navigation electronics, lighting, alternators, starter motors. Qualified auto electrician.

Pre-Purchase Inspections

Independent mechanical assessment before you buy. Engine, electrical, drivetrain, fuel system. Written report within 48hrs.

Fuel & Cooling Systems

Fuel tank cleaning, fuel line replacement, injector service, raw water pump overhaul, heat exchanger service.
