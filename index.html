What We Do

Marine Mechanical & Electrical Services

Full-service mobile marine mechanic covering everything from routine petrol engine servicing to complex diesel rebuilds.

Petrol Engine Service

Oils and filters, spark plugs, impeller and water pump, cooling system flush, fuel system, lower unit gear oil, belts and anodes. Outboards, sterndrives and ski boats — Yamaha, Mercury, Suzuki, Honda, Evinrude.

Diesel Engine Service

Engine oil and filters, fuel filters and water separators, raw water pump impeller, heat exchanger and cooling system, belts, coolant flush, anodes and valve adjustment. Caterpillar, Cummins, Volvo Penta, Yanmar, Perkins.

Marine Electrical

Battery systems, rewiring, navigation electronics, lighting, alternators, starter motors. Marine 12V and 24V diagnosis, testing and repair.

Pre-Purchase Inspections

Independent mechanical assessment before you buy. Engine, electrical, propulsion systems, fuel system. Written report within 48hrs.

Fuel & Cooling Systems

Fuel tank cleaning, fuel line replacement, injector service, raw water pump overhaul, heat exchanger service.

Detailing & Sale-Ready Prep

Cut, polish and wax, gelcoat oxidation removal, stainless and chrome polishing, vinyl and upholstery clean-up, deck wash-down and bilge detail. Selling your boat? A boat that presents well sells faster and for a better price — and because we're mechanics first, we can sort the small mechanical and electrical niggles at the same time, so your vessel both looks right and runs right for buyers and surveyors.

Emergency Callouts

Engine trouble at the marina, on a mooring, or stranded offshore? Priority callouts for urgent mechanical and electrical breakdowns. After-hours available.
