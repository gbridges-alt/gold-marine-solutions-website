You don't need to know which trade it is

You can describe the symptom perfectly and still not know who to ring. The toilet won't flush — is that a plumber? The bilge pump isn't running — is that electrical? The taps have gone quiet — is that the pump or the switch that runs it?

You don't need to identify the correct trade before contacting Gold Marine Solutions. That's our problem to sort out, not yours.

A fault that looks like plumbing can turn out to be a switch, a supply fault or a control circuit, and one that looks electrical can turn out to be a blocked hose. We diagnose both sides. You describe what the boat is doing and we work out what it actually is.

Work out the symptom. We'll work out the system.

How we handle it

We diagnose it

On the vessel, across the physical system and its electrical side, until we know what the fault actually is rather than what it resembles.

We quote it

What needs doing, what it involves and what it costs, before work starts.

We repair or replace it

Where the work falls within Gold Marine's capability, we repair or replace the failed mechanical, plumbing or 12/24V component. Where licensed or specialist work is required, we coordinate the appropriate trade.

We coordinate where it's needed

Some work sits with a licensed specialist — refrigerant handling, 240V shore-power work, or a haul-out where a through-hull has to come out. Gold Marine can help coordinate appropriate services where requested, attend, and confirm the work against the agreed scope. Those specialists remain responsible for their own regulated work, licences and certification.

We document it

We document what was found and what was done. Where relevant, we note follow-up or next-due work.

Get it looked at

Tell us what the boat is doing — the symptom is enough, you don't need to diagnose it first — along with the vessel and where it's berthed, and we'll come back with a time and a quote.
