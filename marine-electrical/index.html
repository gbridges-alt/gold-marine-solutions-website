We find the fault first

A no-start can be mechanical or fuel related, or it can sit in the starting, charging or control circuit. Gold Marine can assess both the mechanical and electrical side of the fault, so the owner does not need to choose the trade before calling.

That matters because the symptom does not identify the cause. An engine that won't crank, a gauge reading incorrectly or a pump that has stopped can each have causes across more than one system, and testing is what settles it.

On one investigation, poor engine grounds were allowing current to return through the bonding circuit — verified with amp-clamp testing.
