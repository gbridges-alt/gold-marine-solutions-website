What's Behind the Memberships

Structured Care, Clearly Defined

Paper-Documented Vessel Service Book

Every Gold Marine member receives a physical vessel service book maintained by Gold Marine Solutions.

This service book is updated as inspections, services and recommendations are completed. It creates a paper-documented maintenance history for the vessel and helps owners track what has been checked, what has been completed and what is coming due.

The service book may include:

  • Completed inspections
  • Service notes
  • Maintenance recommendations
  • Upcoming service intervals
  • Oil sampling records where applicable
  • Faults found
  • Repairs completed
  • Photos where applicable
  • Owner recommendations
  • Annual maintenance planning

A properly maintained service book gives the owner confidence, improves accountability and helps protect resale value.

Bilge System Checks

Bilge system checks are included because bilge pump or bilge switch failure can quickly become a serious issue.

Depending on the membership tier and vessel access, bilge checks may include:

  • Bilge pump operation
  • Manual switch operation
  • Automatic float switch or sensor function
  • High-water alarm check where fitted
  • Visual wiring condition review
  • Bilge cleanliness and water presence
  • Pump discharge confirmation where safe
  • Signs of leaks, contamination or abnormal water ingress

Wash-Downs and Interior Cleans

Included wash-downs and interior cleans are maintenance cleans only. They are designed to maintain presentation, not restore neglected vessels.

Heavy mould, staining, oxidation, teak treatment, stainless restoration, carpet extraction, upholstery restoration, cut/polish correction and major detailing are quoted separately.

Wash-down inclusions are subject to vessel size, condition, water access, weather, marina rules and safe access. Additional time may be quoted separately for larger vessels or heavily soiled vessels.

Wax/Polish Credits

Gold Premier and Gold Signature include annual wax/polish credits. These credits are designed to help maintain exterior protection and presentation.

Cutting, heavy oxidation correction, gelcoat restoration, ceramic coatings, teak treatment and major detailing restoration are quoted separately.

Wax/polish credits are applied toward scheduled exterior protection work and are not redeemable as cash.

Initial Vessel Survey

Gold Premier includes an initial vessel health survey. Gold Signature includes a comprehensive initial vessel management survey.

The purpose of the initial survey is to establish the vessel's current condition, identify immediate concerns, document baseline systems and create a maintenance plan.

The initial vessel survey is a Gold Marine vessel health assessment for membership onboarding and maintenance planning. It is not a certified insurance, valuation or pre-purchase survey unless specifically quoted.

Bonding and Earthing Inspections — Gold Signature

Gold Signature includes scheduled bonding and earthing system inspections. These inspections may include visual checks of bonding connections, earth straps, corrosion points, anode condition, key bonding points and signs of stray-current corrosion.

AC shore-power testing, 240V electrical work and certified electrical repairs are carried out by a licensed marine electrician where required and quoted separately.

Important Membership Boundaries

Memberships include scheduled oversight, reporting and management according to tier.

Repairs, parts, consumables, heavy detailing, cutting, polishing, waxing beyond included credits, antifoul, hardstand, crane/travel lift, subcontractors, skipper services and major corrective works are quoted separately unless included in writing.

Included visits are scheduled service visits and may be time-capped by vessel size and condition.

Priority response is subject to safety, access, weather, parts availability and operational capacity.

Larger vessels, neglected vessels or vessels with known faults may require an onboarding inspection before membership approval.

Gold Signature includes a contractor coordination allowance. Additional project management may be charged hourly. Annual haul-out planning helps organise and manage the process — it does not replace dry docking when it is required.

Membership covers one nominated vessel. Multi-vessel arrangements are quoted separately.
