Annual and 100-hour servicing

Service schedules can include both hour-based and time-based intervals. The correct schedule depends on the specific engine and manufacturer requirements — so the manufacturer's schedule for your particular motor is where we start. How the boat is used and the condition we find can add to what's worth doing, but it doesn't change what the manufacturer specifies. Tell us the engine and its hours and we'll confirm what's due.

Depending on the engine and service interval, servicing can cover four areas:

Engine

Engine oil and filter where applicable, fuel filters, spark plugs and raw-water impeller.

Drive and lower unit

Lower-unit or gearbox oil, trim operation and function.

Cooling and running checks

Cooling system checks, thermostat where appropriate, compression testing where indicated, starting and charging checks.

Controls

Steering and control operation, inspected and reported on.

You get the work done and a record of what was found — including anything that isn't due yet but is worth knowing about.

We service outboards, petrol inboards and sterndrive-powered vessels — mobile service at the berth, home, hardstand or other suitable vessel location.

Fault diagnosis and repairs

When the engine isn't running right, the job is to find out why before anything gets replaced.

We diagnose and repair hard starting and no-start faults, loss of power, rough running, overheating, cooling faults, trim problems and fuel-system faults — using compression testing, electronic diagnostics where the engine supports it, and mechanical testing.

A no-start can be mechanical or fuel related, or it can sit in the starting, charging or control circuit. Gold Marine Solutions can assess both the mechanical and electrical side of the fault. You don't have to choose the trade before you call.
