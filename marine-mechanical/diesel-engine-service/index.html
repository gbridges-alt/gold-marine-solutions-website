Diesel servicing

A marine diesel service is straightforward work done properly, on schedule, with the right parts for that engine.

Depending on the engine and service interval, scheduled servicing can include:

  • Engine oil and oil filter
  • Fuel filters, primary and secondary
  • Raw-water impeller
  • Belts — condition, tension, replacement where needed
  • Cooling system inspection, and coolant condition and level
  • Leaks — fuel, oil, coolant and exhaust
  • Engine mounts
  • Hoses, clamps and connections on the engine
  • Starting and charging checks where relevant
  • Engine condition assessment and a record of what was found

Not every item above applies to every engine at every interval. We confirm the service scope against your actual engine, its hours and the manufacturer's schedule for that model, and quote it before the work starts.

We service at the vessel — at the berth, on the hardstand or on the trailer, whichever suits the boat.

Repairs and fault diagnosis

When something is wrong rather than due, the job starts with finding out what it actually is.

We diagnose and repair: hard starting and no-start faults · rough running, hunting and loss of power · overheating · smoke · oil and coolant leaks · fuel supply and filtration faults · cooling-system faults including impellers, heat exchangers and thermostats · engine mounts · belts, hoses and ancillaries · component replacement across the engine.

A no-start isn't always mechanical

A no-start can be mechanical or fuel related, or it can sit in the starting, charging or control circuit.

Gold Marine Solutions can assess both the mechanical and electrical side of the fault. You don't have to choose the trade before you call.

Fuel injection — what we do on the engine, and what goes to the bench

Two different kinds of work, and it's worth being clear about which is which.

On the engine, we do: injector and injection-pump removal and refit · seals and associated mechanical repair · on-engine diagnosis and testing · mechanical timing of injection pumps · mechanical timing and setup of unit injectors where applicable.

Bench work goes to a specialist: injector bench testing · injection-pump bench testing · calibration · specialist overhaul. That equipment is purpose-built and the work belongs on it. Gold Marine can help coordinate appropriate services where requested.

Twin engines and larger installations

Twin-engine and larger diesel installations change the job. Each engine is serviced against its own requirements and condition, with parts, filters and fluid quantities confirmed for the installation. Access on larger vessels can be tighter than it looks from the cockpit hatch.

Gold Marine works as a marine diesel mechanic on the Gold Coast, servicing and repairing single, twin and larger installations across a range of major marine engine makes.
