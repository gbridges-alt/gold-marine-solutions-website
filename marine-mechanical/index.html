We find the fault first

A propulsion symptom can be mechanical, fuel-related, electrical or control-related. Gold Marine diagnoses the fault rather than asking the owner to choose the trade first.

That matters because the symptom doesn't name the cause. An engine that won't start, one that's down on power, one running hot — each of those symptoms can have causes across more than one system, and Gold Marine can assess both the mechanical and electrical side of the fault, so you don't need to choose the trade before calling.

On a recent no-start investigation on a Ford Lehman diesel, compression testing measured all cylinders below 100 psi.
