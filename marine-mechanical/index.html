Real work

Ford Lehman 120 — engine replacement

Excessive smoke, diagnosed as low compression on cylinder six. The engine was disassembled in the boat on the water, craned out from the dock, then replaced with a rebuilt Ford Lees and reassembled in the vessel.

Ford Lehman SP160 — no-start diagnosis

Called out to a no-start. Cranking was fast, and the fuel rack was found stuck closed in the injection pump. Rather than strip the pump on that alone, we compression tested first — and measured under 100 psi on every cylinder.

Volvo Penta petrol — seized engine and repower

Failed manifolds and risers left the engine seized. Neglected servicing turned what should have been a maintenance item into a full repower, and the engine was removed and replaced with a newer unit.

Volvo Penta D2-55 — delivery valve reseal

Fuel leaking into the bilge, traced to the delivery valve housing on the injection pump. Lines, delivery valves and the housing were removed, new O-rings fitted, and the assembly reinstalled.

Caterpillar 3126 — overhead service

Scheduled overhead service, covering the valve set and injector plunger depth.

Mercury Verado 300 — control reprogramming

The helm controls were working back to front. The direction was reprogrammed and the controls set up to operate correctly.
